## What I Learned in CNT 3004

Through CNT 3004, I was able to get an idea of how a network can be considered a system as opposed to a system of protocols. I was able to understand how I could find out where a problem is through the OSI and TCP/IP models by tracking the data flow from the application layer to the physical layer. I understood how addresses and subnets play a role in organizing networks and also got to know the tradeoffs involved when using TCP and UDP. I was also able to understand how wireless networks, VLANs and HTTP relate to everyday communication and network security through understanding the network traffic.

## How My Skills Improved This Semester

At the beginning of the term, I was familiar with several networking vocabulary terms, but I did not feel confident in my ability to explain the relationship between those terms or apply them to solve problems. My comfort level increased in dealing with subnetting problems, interpreting Wireshark packet captures, and determining which layer of the connection required attention. The most significant change that occurred in my learning process was the ability to use evidence to figure out the cause of the problem.

## How This Course Contributes to My Future Career

Networking forms the basis of the security and infrastructure-related fields I would like to pursue. During the process of penetration testing, knowledge of protocols and the boundaries of the network will assist me in identifying the exposed services and the significance of my findings. In the case of blue teaming, networking knowledge will enable me to analyze abnormal traffic and differentiate between normal network behavior and potential attack traffic. In terms of the infrastructure field, networking will assist me in designing reliable and secure networks. The CNT 3004 course enhanced my foundation in all these fields.
