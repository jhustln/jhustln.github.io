After graduating from UCF with my B.S. in Information Technology in 2028, my goal is to work in offensive security, ideally as a **penetration tester** or **red team operator**. Competing on UCF's Collegiate Penetration Testing Competition team has shown me that I enjoy the full engagement process: scoping an environment, finding vulnerabilities, chaining them together, and then explaining the risk and how to fix it in a way a client can act on.

I also want to stay well-rounded. Training as a Windows blue-team specialist for CCDC and building infrastructure for Hack@UCF taught me that understanding how systems are defended and deployed makes me a better attacker, and the other way around. Long term, I'd like to grow into a role where I can lead engagements and mentor newer people, the same way I've helped run beginner-friendly competitions and workshops at Hack@UCF.

**Areas I'm working toward:**
- **Web application and network penetration testing**: building on CTFs, HackTheBox, and competition experience
- **Networking**: deepening what I learned in CNT 3004 through my Proxmox homelab
- **DevOps and cloud infrastructure**: Docker, Terraform, Ansible, and OpenStack, so I understand the environments I'm testing
- **Blue teaming, Wi-Fi hacking, hardware hacking, and AI in security**: topics I want to explore further
